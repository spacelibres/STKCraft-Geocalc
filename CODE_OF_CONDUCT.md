# Code of conduct

Participation must be respectful and free of harassment or discrimination. Discuss technical disagreements using reproducible evidence. Credit others, protect private information, and give specific constructive feedback.

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, personal attacks, sexualized conduct and sustained disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation. Contact a maintainer privately to report conduct concerns; do not publish private information.

These expectations apply to repository discussions, issues, reviews and other project-managed spaces.
